COURTYARD — Free PSX Chinese-Inspired Arena

A stone fighting court, timber balconies, tiled roofs and a painted mountain horizon. COURTYARD is a free environment asset pack by najjar320 for personal and commercial projects.

Stage a courtyard duel, build a compact encounter map, or use the nine separate buildings in your own environment. The supplied Blender scene includes Eevee lighting and a camera looking across the combat stage.

What you get

  • Nine distinct buildings with a mix of gables, lean-tos and four-sided pyramid roofs.
  • Separate arena floor, connecting streets and stairs: 12 environment mesh objects in total.
  • A separate 2D mountain-and-sky backdrop, included in both Blender scenes and supplied as a PNG and its own GLB.
  • One baked atlas for each environment object, from 512 × 512 to 2048 × 2048. The arena and street each have their own 2048 × 2048 atlas.
  • Editable Blender scenes, GLB exports, PNG textures, Building Topology and Building Texturing sources, regeneration scripts and preview images.

Simple geometry, painted detail

The main environment is 7,444 triangles. Screens, timber grain, carving bands, roof tile courses, repaired plaster and wear are painted into the object atlases. Shallow balconies, a recessed stair entrance, raised street edges and broad roof eaves add depth to the silhouettes.

The building footprint is approximately 40 × 34 metres before roof overhangs. Street edges are 10 centimetres wide; the arena walkway is 1 metre wide. Openings connect the stair approach and side streets.

Start using it

  1. Download and extract the ZIP.
  2. Open source/combat_stage_eevee.blend for the combat composition, or source/combat_fight_arena_psx.blend for the overview scene.
  3. For your own engine, import exports/combat_fight_arena_psx.glb. Import exports/mountains_sky_backdrop.glb separately if wanted.
  4. Use nearest texture filtering for the intended pixel look.

FAQ

Is this a playable game? No. It is static environment art. Characters, combat logic, collision meshes and navigation are not included.

Are the buildings furnished? No. The pack focuses on exteriors; window detail is painted.

What has been checked? Blender 3.5.1 Eevee rendering and GLB reimport, including meshes, atlases and UVs. Other engines have not been tested.

How was it made? Local Building Topology, Boneglass, Building Texturing and Blender tools with AI-assisted scripts and artwork. The mountain-and-sky background was generated with an image model. Supplied renders show the actual Blender assets.
